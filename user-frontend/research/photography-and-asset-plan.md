# Photography & Asset Plan — Patidar Stone Crusher

## Phase 1 (launch, no real photos yet)

- Use **licensed stock** (Unsplash/Pexels/Paid stock) of: aggregate stockpiles, crusher machinery, conveyor/screening, tipper trucks being loaded, close-up texture of crushed stone and sand.
- Label honestly: "Representative image — actual plant photos coming soon" as a small caption, or use abstract/texture images in decorative roles only. **Never caption stock as our plant.**
- Neutral placeholders (plain color blocks / icon + label cards) for: the plant page hero, team, and anything factual.
- Design leans on typography, material-palette colors (stone grey, sand ochre, dust beige), and texture crops rather than fake realism.

## Phase 2 — Photography brief (shoot on one clear day, phone camera acceptable)

| # | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wide plant exterior | Shows scale; shoot morning light |
| 2 | Jaw/cone crusher | Static + short video clip |
| 3 | Conveyor & screening | Material flowing if possible |
| 4 | Gitti stockpiles | One per size, with context (person/truck for scale) |
| 5 | M-sand pile + hand-scoop close-up | Texture sells quality |
| 6 | Stone dust pile | Separate from gitti visually |
| 7 | Loading operation | Tipper being loaded at the chute |
| 8 | Trucks/dispatch | Loaded trucks at gate |
| 9 | Material close-ups | 6/12/20 mm on neutral surface with a scale reference (coin/ruler) |
| 10 | Staff/operations | Only with consent; owner preferred for the About page |

Practical: landscape orientation, wipe the lens, avoid harsh noon sun, take 3–4 shots per item, keep originals; the site will compress.

## Asset checklist for launch

- Logo / wordmark (simple text lockup acceptable), favicon.
- Owner portrait (Phase 2), location pin map embed, 6–10 stock images.
