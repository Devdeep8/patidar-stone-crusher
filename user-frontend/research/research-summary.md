# Research Summary — Patidar Stone Crusher, Suwasra (MP)

*Date: 2026-10-23. All findings from public web search. Verified facts are cited; hypotheses are labeled.*

## 1. Market context

- **Location**: Suwasra, Mandsaur district, Madhya Pradesh, near the Rajasthan border. The commercially relevant catchment is Mandsaur district and adjacent corridors (Bhanpura, Sitamau, Neemuch direction, Ratlam).
- **Transport economics**: Aggregate is a low-value-per-tonne commodity; haul distance dominates landed cost. Realistic service radius is roughly 40–60 km by road before freight makes the material uncompetitive (hypothesis — standard industry rule of thumb; no local freight table found publicly).

## 2. Demand indicators (verified, public sources)

- Suwasra–Guradiakala road widening/upgradation (~22.5 km) awarded to M/S Dhundhwal Brothers ([newprojectinformation.com](https://newprojectinformation.com/m-s-dhundhwal-brothers-got-a-project-widening-and-upgradation-of-suwasra-guradiakala-road-via-angari-road)).
- Suwasara–Bhanpura Highways Pvt Ltd — MPRDC PPP road project connecting Suwasra and Bhanpura (search-result summary; verify on MPRDC site).
- MP cleared a ~82 km HAM road in the Mandsaur region near the Rajasthan border.
- Delhi–Mumbai Expressway passes through Mandsaur/Ratlam districts (244.5 km in MP).
- PWD cross-drainage/flood-damage repair tenders near Suwasra (~₹11.88 crore).

**Conclusion**: sustained road and infrastructure demand for aggregate and M-sand around Suwasra — high confidence.

Regional 20 mm retail range ₹620–850/t; Bhopal live rate for stone dust ~₹823/t (Infralens). **Do not publish prices as our own — cite only as market context.**

**Opportunity**: a proper website with product pages, plant evidence, and click-to-call/WhatsApp would be the *first* in the local competitive set — strong first-mover advantage for organic local search.

## 4. Buyer behavior

- Aggregates in this belt are bought through **phone/WhatsApp, IndiaMART enquiries, Justdial calls, and dealer relationships**. Prices are negotiated per order, not published (Instagram dealer posts like "gitti rate … contact for best price" confirm the informal pattern).
- Buyers ask: what sizes/grades, rate per tonne/brass, minimum quantity, delivery or pickup, distance/freight, payment terms.

## 5. Implications (top 3)

1. Build a small, deep site around **product + locality + trust** (plant, process, sizes), not price.
2. Primary conversion = **click-to-call + WhatsApp enquiry** with a short form (material, quantity, delivery location, date).
3. Own the local search terms ("gitti supplier Suwasra", "M-sand Mandsaur") that no competitor currently serves.
