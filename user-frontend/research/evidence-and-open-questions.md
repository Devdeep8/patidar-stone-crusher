## Owner questions that MUST be resolved before launch

1. Which aggregate sizes/grades are actually produced (6/10/12/20/40 mm? GSB?)?
2. Is genuine M-sand produced, or crusher dust sold as sand? (Marketing must not overstate.)
3. Plant capacity and machinery — what can be truthfully stated?
4. Own transport or buyer-arranged? Realistic delivery radius?
5. Licenses in hand: mining lease/consent to operate/GST — displayable?
6. Operating hours; phone number(s) for the site; WhatsApp number.
7. Preferred domain name.
8. Any existing customers willing to give a reference or review?
9. Photography session feasible? When?
10. Who responds to enquiries and how fast?

## Design brief (one paragraph)

Mobile-first, fast, 8-page static Next.js site in stone/sand natural tones. Above the fold on every page: phone + WhatsApp. Three product pages sized around "what is it, what sizes, how supplied, enquire now". A plant page that sells trust once photos exist. Honest, plain Hinglish copy; zero invented claims. The site's only job: turn a Google search for gitti/M-sand near Suwasra into a phone call or WhatsApp message.
