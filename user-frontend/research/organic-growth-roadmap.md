# Organic Growth Roadmap — Patidar Stone Crusher

Prioritized by impact × evidence strength ÷ effort.

## Month 0 — Foundation (highest impact, low effort)

1. Buy domain (suggest `patidarstonecrusher.in` or `.com`; owner decides).
2. Build the 8-page site (see sitemap) with stock-imagery contingency.
3. Create **Google Business Profile** — biggest local discovery channel given competitors live on directories; verify address, hours, phone, category "Sand & gravel supplier".
4. List on Justdial, IndiaMART, IDBF Mandsaur — where verified competitors get discovered today.
5. GA4 + Search Console; conversion events (call, WhatsApp, form).

## Months 1–2 — Content & local proof

6. Publish 4 articles from the keyword map (M-sand vs river sand, gitti sizes, how rates are decided, gitti per sq ft).
7. Ask 2–3 repeat contractor customers for GBP reviews (honest only).
8. Real photography session → replace all stock (see photography plan).

## Months 3–6 — Validation & expansion

9. Review GSC: double down on queries with impressions; merge pages with zero.
10. Add FAQ sections with FAQPage schema on product pages.
11. Only if delivery is real: add a Mandsaur/Bhanpura coverage mention or page (no town-page farming).
12. Hindi/Hinglish landing variants for top queries if GSC shows Hindi impressions.

## Ongoing (monthly, ~2 hrs)

- GBP post (dispatch activity, monsoon/seasonal notes).
- 1 article or page update.
- Review lead log: material mix, enquiry→order rate, response time.

## KPI targets (12 months, indicative)

- Rank #1–3 for "stone crusher Suwasra" / brand terms (high confidence, zero competition).
- Page 1 for "gitti supplier Suwasra/Mandsaur" cluster (medium confidence).
- Trackable: GBP calls/month, WhatsApp taps/month, form leads/month.
