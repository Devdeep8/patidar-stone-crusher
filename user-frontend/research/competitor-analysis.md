## Key reverse-engineering findings

1. **Nobody in the local set has a website.** The entire "search-intent surface" (product + locality pages) is unoccupied. First-mover content advantage is available at near-zero competitive cost.
2. **Directories are the discovery layer.** Justdial/IndiaMART/IDBF listings rank and get calls. PSC must be listed there *and* outrank them with its own pages.
3. **IndiaMART sellers publish prices; local crushers don't.** A "rates on enquiry with transparent factors" approach (distance, quantity) differentiates without locking into prices.
4. **Trust gap**: none of the local competitors show plants, machinery, process, or coverage. Authentic photography + manufacturing detail is the biggest differentiator available.

## Positioning implication

Do not copy any competitor's wording or identity. Position PSC as: **"Suwasra's own crushing plant — gitti, M-sand, and stone dust direct from the manufacturer, delivered to your site."**
