# Customer Personas — Patidar Stone Crusher

*Evidence base: public directory listings, IndiaMART/Justdial patterns, tender records. Behavioral details are evidence-informed hypotheses — no private interviews conducted.*

## 1. Civil / road contractor (primary)

- **Trigger**: wins a road, canal, or building tender (verified local examples: Suwasra–Guradiakala road, PWD repairs). Needs continuous, on-spec supply for the project duration.
- **Order**: recurring bulk — tens to hundreds of tonnes per week; 20 mm / 40 mm gitti, dust for GSB/wmm.
- **Decision criteria**: rate + freight, consistent gradation, daily supply capacity, credit terms after relationship builds.
- **Questions**: sizes available? rate per tonne at site? can you load X trucks/day? royalty/invoice paperwork?
- **Queries**: "stone crusher Suwasra", "gitti supplier near me", "20mm aggregate supplier Mandsaur".
- **Contact**: phone/WhatsApp to plant owner; sometimes visits the plant.
- **Qualifying info needed**: project, location pin, quantity/day, duration, sizes.

## 2. Builder / construction company (secondary)

- **Trigger**: starting a slab, plastering, or concrete phase.
- **Order**: M-sand, 12/20 mm gitti, dust; single or repeat loads.
- **Decision criteria**: M-sand quality (silt, gradation) vs river sand, timely delivery, honest weight/measurement.
- **Queries**: "M-sand manufacturer Mandsaur", "M sand price", "crushed sand vs river sand".
- **Contact**: WhatsApp preferred; wants a same-day rate quote.

## 3. Material dealer / trader (secondary)

- **Trigger**: fulfilling orders from contractors across multiple sites; buys at plant rate and resells.
- **Order**: bulk, price-sensitive, negotiates hard on rate and load scheduling.
- **Questions**: plant-gate rate, monthly capacity, allocation preference, payment cycle.
- **Contact**: phone; relationship-driven repeat business.

## 4. Individual house builder (occasional, low priority)

- **Trigger**: home construction phase; 1–3 truckloads total.
- **Decision criteria**: total delivered price, trust in measurement, simple advice on what material to use.
- **Queries**: "gitti rate aaj", "patthar gitti near me", Hindi-language searches.
- **Contact**: call/WhatsApp; often found via Justdial or word of mouth.
- **Commercial relevance**: low individually, but high search volume and good for word-of-mouth — keep the site friendly to them without over-investing.
