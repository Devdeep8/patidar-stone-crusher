PATIDAR STONE CRUSHER — PRINT FILE GUIDE

patidar_primary.svg: Main full-colour vector logo, black + orange, no raster content.
patidar_primary_transparent.png: 4000 px transparent PNG.
patidar_mono.svg: Solid black version for stamps, invoice printing, etc.
patidar_mono_transparent.png: Solid black transparent PNG.
patidar_reverse.svg: White + orange transparent vector for dark backgrounds.
patidar_reverse_transparent.png: White + orange transparent PNG.
patidar_icon_only.svg: Small-sized stone/P emblem by itself.
brand_preview.png: Three versions shown against the appropriate backgrounds.
actual_size_2cm_300dpi.png: Entire full logo at 2 cm wide, print-resolution test.
actual_size_2cm_zoom_4x.png: Nearest-neighbour enlarged diagnostic view.

TYPE: Devanagari lettering is typed correctly as पाटीदार स्टोन क्रशर,
rendered in Noto Sans Devanagari Black, then converted to vector outlines.
No fonts are embedded or required to print these SVGs.

PRODUCTION CAUTION: Have a fluent Hindi reader approve the physical proof
before mass printing. At only 2 cm total width, the full bilingual tagline
is too small for reliable reading. Use the icon-only version for tiny marks
or place a large wordmark on invoices/signs.

COLORS: Orange #FF6508; near-black #1B1B1B; white #FFFFFF.

All vector paths are scalable; do not convert the SVG to low-resolution
bitmaps when sending to a truck painter or flex-board vendor.
