# Product Context

## Product Overview
Mumbai-based boat charter service offering luxury speed boats and yachts for harbour cruises, island transfers, and special events. Focus on providing memorable experiences on Mumbai's waters with premium vessels and professional service.

## Business Model
- **Lead Generation**: No online transactions - all bookings connect via WhatsApp
- **Target Markets**: Tourism (Elephanta, Mandwa/Alibaug), Events (weddings, corporate), Film production
- **Service Area**: Mumbai Harbour, Mandwa (Alibaug), Elephanta Islands
- **Conversion Channel**: Direct WhatsApp messaging for inquiries and bookings

## Core Offerings

### Primary Packages
1. **Speed Boat Mumbai Harbour Cruise** - Scenic city tours from water
2. **Speed Boat Ride to Mandwa (Alibaug)** - Quick transfers to popular destination
3. **Yacht Hire for Mandwa (Alibaug)** - Luxury yacht transfers
4. **Yacht Hire for Harbour Cruise** - Premium harbour experience on yachts
5. **Premium Guided Tour Packages for Elephanta Tours** - Heritage island tours with guides

### Event Services
- **Boat/Yacht Hire for Film Shoots** - Production support for maritime filming
- **Wedding in Alibaug Speed Boat** - Wedding guest transfers and experiences
- **Corporate Events in Alibaug** - Corporate retreat and event transportation

## Key Requirements
- **SEO Priority**: Full search engine optimization for Mumbai/Alibaug boat charters
- **Package Display**: Clear showcase of 4-5 main packages with details
- **WhatsApp Integration**: Primary contact and booking method
- **Mobile-First**: Most users will browse on mobile before booking
- **Trust Signals**: Safety, professionalism, experience credentials

## Technical Stack
- **Framework**: Next.js 16 (React 19, App Router)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS 4
- **Deployment**: Docker containerized

## Content Priorities
- Location-specific SEO (Mumbai, Alibaug, Mandwa, Elephanta)
- Service type keywords (speed boat, yacht, charter, cruise)
- Event-specific landing potential (wedding, corporate, film)
- Visual showcase of vessels and experiences
